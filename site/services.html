Exterior Detailing

4 Stage Exterior Detail Process

We specialise in surface damage that may need our 4 stage process. Wether brush marks, spider webbing, oxidised dull or just plain neglected paint work we have a process to fix most situations. All you need to do is speak to our operators and work out a course of action to suit your vehicles needs.

  • Vehicle is pressure washed firstly to remove lose dirt and grim.
  • We then hand wash the vehicle with car shampoo.
  • The vehicle is then pressure rinsed.
  • We then clay bar the paint work to deep clean all grim and foreign matter away.
  • Machine cut the paint work to rid of scratches, spider webbing and any dullness to bring back the gloss back.
  • Machine glaze the paint work to de swirl the surface and put back a deep gloss.
  • Finally hand polish with either a quality liquid polish, wax or even both to seal and protect the paint work from the everyday elements.

Paint correction

Conducted in our specialty studio created for these applications. Paint Correction is a method applied to duco (paint work) surfaces to eliminate most imperfections including:

  • Swirl Marks caused by a poor cut & polish.
  • Environmental Damage is a build-up of pollution, rain and sun damage caused by having your vehicle exposed to the elements and not garaged.
  • Marks, scuffs and scratches caused by general day to day usage of your vehicle The method we use are tailored to the severity, depth of damage, colour and the type of duco the auto manufacture have applied to the vehicle.

3 Stage process.

Full Cut, Glaze and Polish. This will get rid of your fine marks, blemishes, scuffs and your general wear and tear.

4 Stage process

(Including stage 3 process)

3000 Grade Trizak with a Cut, Glaze and Polish. Which will get rid of the heavier scratches.

5 Stage

(Including stage 3 & 4 process)

1500 Grade, 3000 Grade Trizak with a Cut, Glaze and Polish. This will remove bird dropping burns and equivalent damage types, where the damage has not affected the clear coat.

6 Stage

Difficult colours require the extra support of a 5000 Grade Trizak after the 1500/3000. The vehicle is always washed first followed by a clay bar to remove any contaminates and inpurities to leave a smooth clean surface to apply the stages required.

After an initial assessment, the experienced technician will tailor the best suited application for your vehicle in our specialty studio.
