Our services

Everything from a wash to a full correction

We can accommodate any request you have for your vehicle and put together a package that suits your needs and budget — whether that's a full detail, an exterior or interior detail, or even just a wash.

01

Mini Detail

A pressure wash, shampoo and rinse, wheels and under-guards degreased, chamois dried, interior vacuumed and windows cleaned inside and out.
02

Full Detail

The complete service. Engine bay degreased, clay bar, hand polish, full interior extraction and leather conditioned.
03

Interior Detail

Specialised products and equipment across every interior surface — carpet, mats, seats, wheel, dash, console, tints and mirrors.
04

Exterior Detail

A four stage process for brush marks, spider webbing, oxidised, dull or plain neglected paint work.
05

Overspray Removal

Industrial fallout, paint overspray, concrete, acid rain and tree sap lifted off your duco.
06

Ceramic Coatings

Graphene Pro 10H N1, Quartz 9H Pro and Quartz 9H — industrial-grade polymer sealers that chemically bond to your paint.
07

Paint Correction

Three to six stage correction in our specialty studio, matched to the depth of the damage and the type of duco.
