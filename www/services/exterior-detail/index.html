Exterior Detail

We specialise in surface damage that may need our 4 stage process. Whether brush marks, spider webbing, oxidised dull or just plain neglected paint work, we have a process to fix most situations.

Request a quote Call 1300 132 750

4 stage exterior detail process

  • Vehicle pressure washed first to remove loose dirt and grime
  • Hand washed with car shampoo, then pressure rinsed
  • Clay bar the paint work to deep clean all grime and foreign matter away
  • Machine cut to remove scratches, spider webbing and dullness
  • Machine glaze to de-swirl the surface and put back a deep gloss
  • Hand polish with a quality liquid polish, wax or both to seal and protect

Windows

  • The exterior of the windows can accumulate a build up of environmental pollution that normal window cleaning will not remove. We have a process that cleans the build up off the glass, leaving a smooth polished finish.
