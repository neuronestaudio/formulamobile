Industrial Fallout Removal

Industrial fallout is airborne metal, dust and grit that embeds itself into the surface of your paint, most often near industrial sites, rail lines and construction.

Book a detail Call 1300 132 750
01

What it does to paint

The particles embed and then corrode, leaving orange or rust-coloured specks that will not wash off.

02

Why washing does not work

The contamination sits in the clear coat rather than on it, so it needs a decontamination process rather than more washing.

03

Part of a wider problem

Fallout usually comes with other airborne contamination — chemical deposits, acid rain and concrete dust.

Will it come back?

Not from the same event. If the vehicle is parked in the same place, a coating gives the paint far more resistance to it.
