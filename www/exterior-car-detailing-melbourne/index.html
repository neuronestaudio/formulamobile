Exterior Car Detailing Melbourne

We specialise in surface damage that may need our 4 stage process — brush marks, spider webbing, oxidised dull or just plain neglected paint work.

Book a detail Call 1300 132 750
01

The four stages

Pressure wash to remove loose dirt and grime, hand wash with car shampoo and pressure rinse, clay bar to deep clean grime and foreign matter, then machine cut, machine glaze and hand polish.

02

Standard exterior detail

Vehicle washed and clay barred, wheels and under guards degreased and pressure washed, engine bay degreased and dried with compressed air, chamois dried, hand polished, tyres glossed and plastics sealed.

03

Glass build-up

The exterior of windows accumulates environmental pollution that normal cleaning will not remove. We have a process that lifts it off and leaves a smooth polished finish.

My paint is past it. Is it worth doing?

Usually. We have a process to fix most situations — speak to our operators and we will work out a course of action.
